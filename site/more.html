More languages

This site runs the thirteen on its poster. There are many more, and these are the places to find them.

Where to look

Esoteric languages are a community hobby, and most of them are documented by the people who made them. The Esolang wiki keeps a list of thousands, each with a description and often a Hello World. The Wikipedia article is a shorter way in.

  • Esolang wiki: Language list
  • Wikipedia: Esoteric programming language
