What it is

Malbolge is named after Malebolge, the eighth circle of hell in Dante's Inferno. Its memory holds numbers in base 3, its only arithmetic is a deliberately strange operation on base-3 digits, and every instruction is scrambled through a substitution table as soon as it has run.

It was hard enough that the first working programs were not written by a person. They were found by a computer search.
