Why it is here

Every other language on this site makes programming harder, stranger or funnier on purpose. Scratch makes it easier on purpose. It is the control in the experiment: proof that looking unusual is not what makes a language esoteric.

In Scratch a program is built by snapping blocks together, so a beginner cannot make a typing mistake. Its Hello World is {{blocks}} blocks: an event block that starts the script when the green flag is clicked, and a block that makes a sprite say the line.

Esoteric or not

QuestionEsoteric languagesScratch
Who is it for?Programmers, as a puzzle, a joke or an argumentBeginners, often children
Is hard meant to be hard?Often yes (Malbolge, Brainfuck)No: blocks only fit where they make sense
Is the strange form the point?Yes: images (Piet), invisibility (Whitespace)No: the blocks exist to remove syntax errors
