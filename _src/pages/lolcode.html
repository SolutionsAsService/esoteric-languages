What it is

LOLCODE is esoteric because of its joke, not its difficulty: its keywords are written in the broken English of lolcat captions. Under the joke it is an ordinary language with variables, loops and functions, which is why its Hello World takes a single statement.

This site runs a small part of it: the statements on this page.

The keywords used here

KeywordMeaning
HAI 1.2Start of the program, and the language version
VISIBLEPrint
I HAS A n ITZ 0Declare n with the value 0
IM IN YR loop UPPIN YR n TIL …Loop, adding 1 to n each time, until the condition holds
BOTH SAEM n AN 3n equals 3
IM OUTTA YR loopEnd of the loop
