What it is

Rockstar is a language whose programs look like rock lyrics, and are meant to be sung. It is esoteric in its aim, not its difficulty: under the lyrics it is an ordinary language with variables, loops and arithmetic, with some of the ordinary features given a rock-and-roll spelling.

Two of these are the point. A poetic number is written as a sentence, and its digits are the lengths of the words: a lean mean wrecking machine is 14487. A poetic string is written with says, and everything after it is the text. Variables can have names like Tommy, the music or Doctor Feelgood.

This site runs the part in the table. Conditionals, functions, arrays and the rest are not run here.
