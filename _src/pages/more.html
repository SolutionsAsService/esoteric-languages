More languages

Two more, described but not run: this site has no interpreter for them yet, so it shows no program and no step count. Chef, Shakespeare, Unlambda and FALSE used to be here; they now have pages of their own.

INTERCAL

Don Woods and James M. Lyon, 1972: the first esoteric language, written as a parody of the languages of its day. Statements can be marked PLEASE, and the compiler rejects a program that is not polite often enough, or one that is too polite.

Rockstar

Dylan Beattie, 2018. Programs are written to read like the lyrics of a power ballad.
