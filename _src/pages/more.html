More languages

Six more, described but not run: this site has no interpreter for them yet, so it shows no program and no step count.

INTERCAL

Don Woods and James M. Lyon, 1972: the first esoteric language, written as a parody of the languages of its day. Statements can be marked PLEASE, and the compiler rejects a program that is not polite often enough, or one that is too polite.

  • Esolang wiki: INTERCAL
  • Wikipedia: INTERCAL

Shakespeare

Karl Wiberg and Jon Åslund, 2001. A program is a play: the characters are variables, acts and scenes are places to jump to, and insults and compliments are numbers.

  • Esolang wiki: Shakespeare
  • Wikipedia: Shakespeare Programming Language

Chef

David Morgan-Mar, who also made Piet and Ook!. A program is a recipe: ingredients hold values and mixing bowls are stacks.

  • David Morgan-Mar: Chef
  • Esolang wiki: Chef

Unlambda

David Madore, 1999. A functional language with nothing but function application: no variables, and very few built-in functions.

  • Esolang wiki: Unlambda
  • Wikipedia: Unlambda

FALSE

Wouter van Oortmerssen, 1993. A tiny stack language with a very small compiler. It inspired Brainfuck.

  • Esolang wiki: FALSE

Rockstar

Dylan Beattie, 2018. Programs are written to read like the lyrics of a power ballad.

  • Esolang wiki: Rockstar
  • Rockstar: the official site
