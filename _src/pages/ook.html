What it is

Ook! has exactly the eight commands of Brainfuck, each written as a pair of the words Ook., Ook? and Ook!. Any Brainfuck program can be translated word for word, so the two run the same number of steps.

Its designer, David Morgan-Mar, also made Piet. The words are the Librarian’s, from Terry Pratchett’s Discworld.
