# Critic: Formalist (Arnheim), R2

**Anchor used:** Arnheim, Art and Visual Perception: balance about the centre and visual weight (brightness, size, isolation), the structural skeleton, direction and force.

**Observed in the work:**
- The skeleton holds. The heavy black rule under the headline, then two rows of coloured fields, carry the same grid as R1. The numerals (906, 906, 98, 27 / 26, 40, 1, 2) still sit at one height in each row, so they remain the primary rhythm.
- Piet is resolved. The bars now run to about x=475 of the field and rise to a magenta terminal stub at upper right. Their ragged tops give a rightward force that closes the black field. R1's dead right half is gone.
- Counterweights are good: Befunge's bars end low-right, Whitespace's grid sits right, Brainfuck's tape sits low-left. The top row is stable.
- Competition: the Malbolge field's white-marked characters (x 555-835, y 690-790) are the brightest large patch in the bottom row. Pale blocks on deep red, and nearby, pull the eye below the white "40". Because the white on the heavy red field attracts, the lower row tilts toward Malbolge's base.
- LOLCODE's white outlined caption (y 680-790) is four lines of heavy capitals. It is large but stays under the black "1" in dominance, since the caption sits below it and is smaller. It is acceptable.
- The eye lands first on the headline, then Brainfuck's red 906, then drifts to the Malbolge white patch.

**Verdict:** PASS. The devices subordinate to the numerals except the Malbolge white marks, which are a mild imbalance, not a structural defect.

**Requested change (one):** Tint the Malbolge changed-character marks (a pale pink or lower-opacity white, not full white) so the white "40" outranks them.

**Statement of meaning:** One grid of eight primary-colour fields in which number leads and program follows.

**Critic question:** Should any single device ever be brighter than its own numeral?
